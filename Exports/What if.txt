What if the mirror’s just lying again,
calling you “less” like it knows your name, 
What if the flaw was never your frame,
just old voices playing a tired game,
and you were enough before your doubt ever came, 

What if the weight was never on your skin,
Using  filters and angles, posing as thin,
Making you question your size over and over again,
Counting every 'loss' as a gain,
Doubting your self, waiting on likes just to feel okay again, 

What if those lives you see on your screen,
Hide the parts where the hurts are seen,
All the lavish you wish you had been in,
battle you know you cant win, by playing clean,
and you've been comparing your truth to a scene,

What if you’re tired of trying to fit in, play pretend,
laughing  louder just to make new friends,
good days turn grey from a text left on 'seen', 
Reading too deep the words in between,
chasing their praise like it pays something due,
lose your own voice tryna sound more approved, 

What if the peace you want isn’t earned,
not in their words or the way they confirm,
not in applause or the tables that turn,
maybe self-worth isn’t something you learn, 
maybe it’s yours, something steady, something sure, 
what if?

Alvin
