Nobody told me it would happen this quietly,
no ceremony, no moment where the music swells.
Just one morning you're tying your shoes and you realize
your hands look like your father's and that's that.

You stop asking for permission and start asking for forgiveness, you stop saying someday and start checking your calendar. The things you thought were boring become routine, and the things you thought were forever become memories.

I miss the version of me that had no concept of time,
who played until it got dark and came home hungry.
Now I track my hours like a man on a deadline,
because I am, we all are, we just don't say it out loud.

Growing up isn't losing your childhood, exactly.
It's more like carrying it behind you, getting heavier each year.
But you don't put it down. You keep walking.
That weight is proof you had something worth remembering.. The reasons why all thats lost isn't actually gone but just out of reach 

Alvin
