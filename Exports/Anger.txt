I used to apologize for it before it even arrived, Pre emptive sorry, shrinking before I was even asked to shrink. Swallowing things that deserved to be said out loud and calling that maturity when it was mostly fear.

Anger isn't the problem, What you do with it is, and I've done both. I've burned things I shouldn't have, and I've buried things that deserved a fire, neither one worked, both just left damage.

I'm finding the middle version, the anger that speaks clearly, not from a wound, not to wound, but to mark a line. This is where I stop. This is what I won't accept. I say it once, calmly, with both feet on the ground.

That's not aggression. That's dignity. That's knowing what matters enough to protect with your voice. I stopped apologizing for the fact that I feel things. Turns out the feeling was never the problem, the hiding was. You don't have to be quiet to be good.
