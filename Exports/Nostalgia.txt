I went back to a place I grew up loving, 
and it looked smaller than I remembered.
Not worse — just smaller. More ordinary.
The magic was in me, not the place, and I'd moved it without knowing.

Memory does that. It upgrades everything slightly.
Softer light, warmer air, everyone kinder.
A past so edited you can't live in it anymore,
only visit, and even then, it's not quite right.

I miss things that may not have been exactly as I miss them.
That's the honest version. The nostalgia is real
but the thing it's pointing to might be as much invention as memory.
I've mourned places that never quite existed.

Still, I don't think that makes it worthless.
The feeling is real, even if the picture isn't perfect.
And the person I was in those moments uncertain, young, alive
deserves to be remembered fondly, even if I've moved on.

Alvin
