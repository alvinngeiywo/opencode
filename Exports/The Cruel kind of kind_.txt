We were just talking, nothing serious,
Late nights, dry jokes, you being mysterious.
You'd send a meme, I'd send one back,
Easy conversation, no pressure, maybe i was in luck. 

You started leaving hearts on random things I said,
I didn't read too much into it, kept a cool head.
Then the texts got longer, warmer, a little more,
Started feeling like something I'd felt before.

You'd say "I miss this" and I'd say i feel the same, 
Neither of us putting a name to the game.
Then you went quiet, back to your life, I suppose 
You already won, why'd you come back just to watch me lose?

Alvin
