One says go, the other says stay,
one whispers you're enough, the other screams you're not, 
I'm being pulled in two directions, losing my way,
but somewhere in the middle, I'm tearing apart.
They're both so loud I can't hear myself, 
should I think or should I pray?

My heart wants one thing, the world wants another,
one voice says chase your dreams, the other says survive first, 
They're killing each other inside my head,
caught between two truths I can't both hold.
So which one do I choose when they're both screaming?

I'm paralyzed by the conflict, can't break free,
stuck between what I love and what's safe,
between who I want to be and who they need me to be, 
Both voices claim they're trying to save me,
but they're both drowning me at the same time.

So maybe the real problem isn't choosing,
maybe I've been listening so hard I forgot I had a voice.
And the scariest part? 
I don't know if my voice would sound any different,
or if I've become them entirely.
