You left long before you walked away, 
yet I kept reaching,
hoping your shadow would finally turn into a father.

Your stories broke open in time, 
truths hidden under all the lies,
pain stitched behind every memory.

But somewhere between the anger
and the empty promises,
 I found something real, me.

Not the boy waiting for you to return, 
but the man learning to stand
Without the weight of your absence.

Mom carried the house, I carried the hurt,
yet here I am, still rising, 
still choosing a heart that doesn’t harden.

Your disappearance didn’t end me.
It revealed me, and the ruins you left behind,
Made me meet the person I was always meant to become.
