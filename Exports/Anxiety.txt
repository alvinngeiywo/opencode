It starts before I have a reason to give it, 
A heartbeat.. A strong sense that something, something is about to go wrong in a way I can't prepare for.. but should probably be preparing for right now.

My brain runs worst case  scenarios like a simulation, 
Not once.. repeatedly, with small variations, very detailed, 
By the time the event arrives I've already survived six versions of disaster
and I'm exhausted from a fight that never happened.

The real events are almost always lighter than the previews. I walk in, it goes fine, I walk out, I breathe. And I think.. all that for this. And I almost laugh. And then the next thing starts loading. 

I'm learning to name it without feeding it. This is anxiety. This is not information. This is noise. I'm learning to let the heartbeat be there without controlling it. It's slow work, but I'm somewhere in the middle of it.
