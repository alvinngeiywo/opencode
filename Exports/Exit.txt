People who leave that way don't escape the pain
they just stop being able to find out they were wrong 
There is no dramatic silence after
No watching people cry at your grave
and feeling seen at last.
Calls itself a gift 
But gifts don't leave people 
Just gone. Just nothing
And somewhere a mother
opens a bedroom door
and the world 
splits in two forever.
Suicide doesn't end suffering.
It sells you silence 
Like silence is the same as peace
Like the noise inside your head 
is proof the world won't grieve. 
The cruelest part 
most people who survive it
say they didn't want to die
They just wanted the hurting to stop
Those are two different things
one of them survives

Alvin
