She left for school but never came back, 
The news said "found" like she had just losther way, 
We told her to be careful, just in case,
Beside the road her was life erased.

They stick her picture on the walls and walk away, 
We scroll past her between a meme and our stories 
We hand out "stay safe" to girls like it's advice,
So sad we've normalized what we ignore.

Her books has tomorrow's homework in it,
Didn't she deserve to fill each empty page? 
We mourn for a week and we call it closure 
Then wonder why we mourn one girl more. 

We get mad, we share, we cry, 
Then switch to some thing lighter 
Election season, her name is used as leverage. 
We speak, march, demand, what else can we do? 
because our silence is their biggest wage
Cause every time we're quiet, they grow bolder.
