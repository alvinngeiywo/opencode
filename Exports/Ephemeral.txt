She's somewhere between real and almost 
close enough to feel,
far enough to question.
I keep finding her in small things,
which is either a sign
or just what hoping looks like from the inside.
Warm one moment, distant the next 
Like the sun, except she decides when to rise 
that could mean anything, 
right? 
I've been solving for x
But she's part of the equation too, 
Cause she keeps changing the value of y 
So i just play along. 
Still, I come back.
Not because i found a way through, no
The most interesting systems
don't let you in easily 
but because uncertain feels like the most alive I've been
I smile either way
Either way I'm here 
which is its own kind of answer.

Alvin
