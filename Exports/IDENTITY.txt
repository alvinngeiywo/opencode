I have performed so many versions of myself
that some days I can't find the original.
The one that existed before the world had opinions
before I learned to adjust based on the room in general. 
I code-switch, shape-shift, edit in real time.
Have this version for home, That one for outside.
Another for people who need me to be smaller.
Another for places where small feels like survival.
It's exhausting. Not in a dramatic way, just steady,
the way carrying a bag all day tires your shoulder.
But you still need to,
You don't notice until you put it down.
I'm still learning how to put it down.
Somewhere underneath all the adjustments is someone real.
Someone who laughs too loud and takes up space unapologetically.
I'm not there yet. But I'm moving towards it.
Or at least I think I am
Validating my self daily is not the way i want it.

Alvin
